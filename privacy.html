Afterstream PF

Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Afterstream PF is a personal tool run by the streamer Powerless Freddy to post clips from his own Twitch streams to his own TikTok account. It has one user: its owner.

1. What it collects

When the owner connects his TikTok account, TikTok gives Afterstream PF a login key (an access token) and basic account details such as the display name. Nothing is collected from anyone else, including viewers or followers.

2. How it is used

The login key is used only to upload videos the owner has approved, with their captions, to the owner's own account.

3. Where it is kept

The login key is stored only on the owner's own computer. Afterstream PF has no servers and no database, and it does not send this information anywhere except to TikTok when posting.

4. Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone. There are no ads, analytics or tracking.

5. Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings under security and permissions. Deleting the stored key on the computer removes it completely.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

7. Contact

Questions: reach Powerless Freddy through twitch.tv/powerlessfreddy.
